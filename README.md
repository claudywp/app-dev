🎬 The Hunger Games (2012)
Overview

The Hunger Games is a dystopian science-fiction action film directed by Gary Ross. It adapts the 2008 novel by Suzanne Collins and was released in March 2012. It launched one of the biggest young-adult film franchises of the 2010s.

In the nation of Panem, each district must send two teenagers to fight to the death on live television.

Main Cast

**bold text** Plot Summary
Katniss volunteers as tribute after her younger sister, Prim, is chosen at the Reaping.
She and Peeta travel to the Capitol to train and meet their mentors.
They compete in the Games, an arena fight where only one tribute can survive.
Their bond with each other challenges the Capitol’s rules.
Key Themes
Power and control
Inequality between the Capitol and the districts
Reality TV as a tool of oppression
Sacrifice and survival
